    02 / Quick tools

    Find a reset
    that fits.

    Pick one thing that feels doable. Skip anything that doesn’t feel right for you.

    ◌ 01◌

    Take a slower breath

    Give yourself a short pause. Follow the circle if that feels comfortable.

    Ready?

    A simple breathing pause

    Breathe normally if focusing on your breath feels uncomfortable.

    ◎ 02✳

    Come back to now

    Notice what’s around you, one sense at a time. Take your time with each step.

    1. 5 things you can see
    2. 4 things you can feel
    3. 3 things you can hear
    4. 2 things you can smell
    5. 1 thing you can taste

    Take your time. You can skip any step.

    ↗ 03↗

    Untangle one task

    Write down what’s on your mind. Choose a tiny next step, not the whole solution.

    A first step can take just five minutes.

      Notice one sound nearby, then one farther away. No need to label it as good or bad.

      Stay with this moment for as long as feels useful.

        A note from the creator

        Why we made this.

        Developers: Naveen Kumar, Dilshaj Ashrafi, Gaurav Raj, Abhishek Kumar Singh
        Class / course: B3/B210 · BTech CSE (AI)

        We made Mind Care as a student project because looking after your wellbeing can feel confusing, and starting a conversation isn’t always easy. We wanted to make one friendly page with a few simple ways to pause and find help. It’s for everyone, whatever life looks like for you.

        A quick privacy note: Mood history, plans, daily care choices, contacts, feedback, and journal notes are saved only in this browser on this device and may be visible to others who use it. Chat messages are sent to Google Gemini for a reply. In the online language build, fixed interface text (not personal entries) is sent to the configured translation service when you choose a new language. Check translations against official sources before relying on support information. See Google’s AI terms. This is a student project, not professional or emergency care.

        Display settings

        Display choices are saved in this browser.

        Languages, online and offline

        A complete build can include English, Hinglish, and all 22 scheduled Indian languages. The website caches every included language on the first online visit; the Android app can include them in its installation. AI chat needs an internet connection.
